## Everything the helper changes

The helper only changes how pages look and how they are used in your own browser. It sends the same requests the game's own buttons and links send, and only when you click or press a key (plus a few read-only background reads: item data for tooltips, gear durability, the data files). The **Original** layout switch keeps the game's own profile page; the whole helper can be turned off at any time in Tampermonkey.

### On every page

- **Layout switch** (bottom left corner, remembered): `Original` keeps the game's own pages; `Modern` rebuilds the pages the helper knows (right now the profile) with modern CSS. A theme list sits next to it (only "Original Core" for now).
- **Off switch.** Adding `ssh=off` to a game address (`index.php?cmd=profile&ssh=off`) keeps the helper out of that page, to see the game as it is. Needs the loader from version 1.0.1; Tampermonkey updates it by itself, or install it again from the install page.
- **Modern frame** (Modern layout, every page): everything around the page content is made of divs, no tables: the page background is the HD picture, fixed while you scroll (it comes with the helper, `src/media/`); the banner, the two sidebars, the content frame (its top and bottom pictures and its two borders), the bottom banner and the page grid are divs, and each sidebar ends in its own bottom cap; the content's footer picture comes with the helper (`src/media/`), a 12 px gap separates the frame from the bottom banner, and the game's picture addresses are all https; the banner sits flush with the left edge of the columns, where the game's pictures meet, and the content frame grows down to the bottom of the page on a short page, so the background never shows through between the sidebars (on the world page the lower panels grow and the footer picture sits at the bottom). The page content is rebuilt page by page: the profile and the world page are all divs now (the game's own tables stay in the page, hidden, as the source: the profile's controls and the map data are read from them); the other pages are still the game's own tables, in a div of their own. On the world page the title bar, the map, the Active Buffs strip, the Location Details and Local Comms panels and the footer are divs, and they are built again after every move. The **sidebar widgets** (Main Menu, Questions, Game Stats, Kill Logs, Sigma Box, Skills, Faction Info, Support Us) are divs with a title bar, a body and a bottom, and the menu is a list of divs: drag a widget by its title bar (a ghost follows the pointer and a line shows where it will land) to another place, also to the other sidebar; Esc cancels, the arrangement is kept in your browser, and the menu entries light up under the pointer. The game's own pieces (links, forms, the skills box) are moved into the divs, not copied, so they and the game's scripts keep working, and the Original layout puts the game's tables back. When the page's late-running scripts for the Skills box and the menu are lost while the sidebars are built (it happens now and then when you switch pages), the helper notices the empty box and runs them again. The news / tip panel of the banner shows what the game shows there.
- **Own copy of the Open Sans font.** The game's top bar asks for Open Sans from a server that the browser blocks (a CORS error in the console); the copy that comes with the helper (`src/media/`) is used for it instead.
- **Tooltips replaced.** The game's tooltip library could leave a tooltip stuck to the mouse or stalled off-screen. The new one follows the pointer, opens towards the middle of the window, always closes when you leave the item, and looks like the original (same width, colours and text).
- **Item tooltips no longer freeze the page.** The game loaded item data with a blocking request (about 0.3 s of frozen page per item). The helper shows "Loading Stats..." at once and fills it in when the data arrives; answers are kept for a minute.
- **Buff and skill tooltips explain themselves.** Buff icons and the right-hand "Skills" panel now show what the skill does, with every "x% per point" worked out for the skill's level, plus its cost and duration. Built in: all 133 skills of the skill pages and the stim buffs (Scavenger, Micro Generator, Mind Orb; Accelerator only has what the game says about it).
- **Gear comparison.** The tooltip of an item you are not wearing is shown next to the tooltip of the worn item of the same kind (Weapon, Helmet, Weapon Add-On, ...), the one you hover on the side of the pointer. In the hovered item, every Bonus, Enhancement and Resistance is green when it beats the worn item and red when it does not, with the difference in brackets, e.g. `+16 (+5)`; numbers only the worn item has are added as `0 (-6)`. It works on the profile and in shops (the worn items are read once from your profile page and kept for two minutes), and can be turned off on the Config page.
- **Energy tooltip.** A live countdown to the next gain, the energy right now (the hourly gains since the page was loaded are added), and a new **Full in** row with the time it will be full, in your time and in server time. The gain per hour is read from the tooltip, not fixed.
- **Top banner.** Real HP, skill power and the energy maximum written on the bars; a **gear durability bar** under the HP bar, as long as the bar's slot whatever your HP, with a wrench icon in front (all equipped items added up, re-read in the background when a page loads and after actions, hover for the time of the last check). Updated straight away after fights, actions and skills (the numbers follow the Skill Power bar and the energy when you use a skill). The durability is read again after every fight.
  - In the **Modern** layout the banner is built again with divs, each piece placed on its slot of the banner picture (the HP, XP and Skill Power bars, the Flux Crystal and Energy counters, the animated orbs and the news picture), instead of the game's table that misses them by a few pixels. Links and tooltips are the game's own.
- **Side panels.** *Game Stats* and *Kill Logs* move to the left column (option); a stray blank line after the copyright is removed.
- **Config page: "Sigmastorm Helper" tab.** Settings (route preview and click to walk, stop at creatures, delay between steps, slide animation, banner numbers, durability bar, gear comparison, move side panels), save / defaults, **export / import of all the helper's settings** (the options, layout and theme, map zoom, backpack item size, side panels, Action List position) as one piece of text, and the learned map data: how many tiles are known, export, import, forget. Shows the helper's version and build.

### World map

- **A bigger map in place of the game's 5×5 view**: zooms 35 % to 180 % (the mouse wheel zooms on the pointer, the + / − buttons on the player; remembered), drags with the mouse, always centres on you, and the area outside the map is filled with its own wall tiles.
- **Moving without a page reload**: arrows, keys and clicks update the map, your position, the banner and the action list in place. Using a stairway (a new zone) works the same way.
- **Zone bar** with the area name and coordinates, coloured by safe / PvP zone, one row below the direction arrows so nothing covers it, and two buttons in the row above it: **W** opens the zone's page on the Sigma Storm 2 wiki and **G** its page in the game's Guide (the game does not say the Guide's number for an area, so the helper has the Guide's list of 652 areas, `data/realms.json`; the button is hidden for an area that is not in it).
- **Direction pad** round the map (greyed when the game does not allow a direction) and **hotkeys** Q W E / A D / Z X C for the eight directions.
- **Tile tooltips**: hover a tile for the game's own tile description. Its picture is loaded from the game's current image server (the game still points at one that is gone, so it shows broken).
- **Path finding**: hover any tile to see the route as a green dotted line with "Go to (x, y) - N steps"; click to walk it step by step. It plans around blocked tiles, re-plans if a step is refused, and stops when you press any key or click the map (option: also stop when creatures are on a tile). **Fast walk** (on by default): the steps your energy pays for (1 per straight step, 2 per diagonal) are sent one after another at Fallen Sword's walking pace (250 ms per straight step, 354 ms per diagonal) without waiting for each page; the map shows the walk at once, the energy number goes down with each step, and the page updates once on arrival or when a step is refused (then it re-plans). A creature cannot stop a fast walk on the way. With Fast walk off: one move request per step, spaced by the delay you set (default 320 ms, minimum 100 ms).
- **Walkable-tile data**: built in for the known areas (`data/tiles.json`) and learned from the game's own arrows while you play; exportable and importable on the Config page.
- **Footprints**: a button that marks the tiles you have walked on (per area).
- **Transit portals**: the Examine Portal button in the Action List opens a dialog with every destination, its minimum level, its cost in credits and a Travel Here button (it asks once more before going), instead of a new page. The travel is sent in the background and the new place is swapped in; a message of the game's shows in a dialog.
- **Faction Inventory**: the game's Tagged Item Report page shows every item of the faction in one list (Faction > Faction Inventory, under Manage, opens it; the Config tab's "Faction Inventory on the Tagged Item Report page" setting, on by default, brings the game's own report back when it is off): the tagged items the members carry and the items in the faction store. Each item shows its type, rarity, quality (in the colour of the tooltip), upgrade level, level, Attack/Damage/Defense/Armor/HP and durability; hovering its picture shows its tooltip. Resting the pointer on a member's name shows their level, class, PvP rating and when they were last active. Filter by search, where it is (the store or a member), type, class (Core, Psi, Battlesuit, Rad, Hardwired, or none), rarity, quality, level, worn or not, set item or not; sort by any column; the Eq column shows what a member wears; recall one item with its link, send it straight to your backpack with Take (it recalls it to the store, then takes it from there), or use the checkboxes to Recall selected or Take selected many at once (the store's own items have a Take link too). What each item is comes from the game's own item data, read once and kept in the browser (by the item, so recalled or taken items stay known; kept 60 days after an item was last seen), so opening the page again only reads the new items (Refresh reads it all again, for a changed quality or durability). The item tooltip also works for items that are carried, not worn (the game asks for them with another item state; the helper tries the others when it gets "not found").
- **Path Finder** (`Path Finder` button at the bottom of the map; setting "Path Finder button on the map"): a draggable panel where you search an area by name or by level (`120`, `100-120`) and it shows the shortest way from where you stand: stairs, world maps and portals, with the tiles, the moves still to walk and the steps after the next one. It follows you as you move. The route data (`route.json`) is downloaded from the [Sigmastorm Path Finder](https://cristimirt.github.io/sspathfinder/) website; `route.js` is a copy of the one of that site.
- **Stairs marked on the map** (setting "Mark the stairs on the map"): a green stairs picture on every stairs tile (the tiles whose picture is in the game's `stairways` folder), or a red "no entry" picture when the area it leads to is for a higher level than the character (the target comes from the Path Finder data, the level from the character page). Your marker stays on top.
- **Profile panels** (Modern layout): drag a panel (Statistics, Inventory, Backpack ...) by its title bar to another place in either column, and use the switch on the title bar to hide or show its content. The arrangement is kept, one for your own profile and one shared by all other players' profiles; "Reset panels" at the top of the page puts everything back.
- **Modern map** (setting, on by default): turn it off to use the game's own map on the world page (the helper's map, click to walk, Path Finder, stairs marks and action list are not used then; takes effect after reloading).
- **The game's new request rules** (token, POST for moves, the quick buttons, the bag and the faction store): the helper sends the page's `_csrf` token and a fresh `_operation` value with its requests, sends moves and stairs as POST, and reads the game's POST forms (quick buttons, bag, components, Faction Inventory).
- **Pages the game sends with broken markup** (the mission page of a quest that has not been started) are put back in the usual structure before the helper reads them, in both layouts; the quest page shows the "not started" line as plain text.
- **The game's messages** ("You need at least 20 energy ...") show their line breaks and links as they are meant (links get the token), and the upgrade-level pictures of item tooltips load from the CDN.
- **Quest pages** are made of divs, and the header picture shows once.
- **Fights no longer reload the world page.** After a fight the header (HP, XP, energy, credits) is updated from the fight's answer and the fight keeps its row in the Action List; the list is only read again by the refresh icon or the **R** key. Only a defeat reads the world again, as the player is taken somewhere else. Every 5 seconds the character page is read once to update the header, the faction chat and online members, the sidebar notices (new logs and messages) and, when the bag or the worn items changed (and after fights), the gear durability, the bag count and the supplies.
- **Evolve page** (Modern layout) is made of divs, with a **Points to spend per click** box (1 up to the points you have, and a Max button): the Add buttons then read *Add +N* and send the game's own form N times, one after the other, showing how many are done. It stops at the first error or warning of the game, and the header and the "Level up" notice in the side panel are updated from the answers. The game's table stays hidden in the page, so the Original layout is untouched.
- **Blacksmith page** (Modern layout) is made of divs too: the worn items in a row with their durability and repair price, and a Repair All button. Every button clicks the game's own form, so the game's confirmation stays. The Original layout is untouched.
- **Engineering page** (Modern layout) is made of divs too. Choosing an item no longer reloads the page: click it, or **drag it onto the slot in the middle**, and only the page content is replaced (click the item in the slot to take it out). The Upgrade button clicks the game's own form.
- **Item Augmentation page** (Modern layout) is made of divs too, and works however it is opened (POST or `index.php?cmd=crafting`). Choosing an item no longer reloads the page: click it, or **drag it onto the slot**, and only the page content is replaced (click the item in the slot to take it out). The augmenter buttons click the game's own forms.

The slider stays under the deposit and the withdraw box, and a deposit or a withdraw no longer reloads the page: the game's own form is sent in the background and the page content, the top bar and the side panels are updated from the answer.
- **Manage Faction page** (Modern layout) is made of divs instead of its eighty nested tables (logo, statistics, bank, structures, members, store): every table is turned into a grid of divs with the same widths, alignment and backgrounds (`layout/tablediv.js`, reusable for other table pages). Links and forms are copies that work like the originals, the Leave Faction question included. Its panels (Faction Logo, Statistics, Bank, Structures, Artifacts, Invites / Requests, Faction Store, Members) can be dragged by their title bar to another place in either column and hidden or shown with the switch on the title bar, like the profile page; the arrangement is remembered. The pages of the Faction Store (the numbers under it) load without reloading the page. The Faction Store is called Faction Inventory here, and its Manage link is Store: it opens the page of the items to store as a pop-up (choose items with the checkboxes or Select all, Store Selected), so the page is not left, and the panel is read again afterwards. ### Action list and combat (world page)

- **Creature kinds** in the Action List: the names that end with (Champion), (Elite), (HK) or (Behemoth) are coloured (silver, yellow, red, purple; setting "Colour creature names by type", with the colours shown there), and each kind has a setting for its attack hotkeys ("Attack hotkeys for Champion creatures" ...): a kind without hotkeys is attacked with a click and the number keys go on with the next creature. The names are bold with a dark outline, and the row has a faint tint in the colour of the kind. The Action List is made of divs now, not a table.

- **Floating Action List** instead of the game's action area: drag it anywhere (position remembered), it scrolls with the page, and it lists everything you can do on the tile. Number keys **1–9** attack the creature with that number; the numbers count only the creatures that have a key (Elite, Champion and the like do not, as in the game), and the number on the attack button is always the key that works. A fight that is over keeps its place in the list, so the creatures below it keep their numbers. The **refresh icon** in the list's header (or the **R** key) reads the tile again; it replaces the game's R, which repaired at the mechanic (the list has a repair-all button for that).
- **Quick combat from the list**, including Elite creatures: the fight runs in the background and the result is written into the row (XP, gold and tax, the hit points you lost (a red heart) and the number of turns (your attacks), each figure is an icon with a tooltip, skill power, level up / down, the loot with the game's item tooltips). A button opens the **combat log** in a draggable pop-up that looks like the game's own fight page (both combatants with their pictures, final hit points and ammo, the victory / defeat marks, the whole log, and the summary of what was gained or lost), made of divs instead of the game's tables and without its two links. When the game refuses an attack (not enough energy, ...), its message shows in a dialog and the page is not reloaded.
- **Ammo refill after every fight**: the ammo the weapon has is read from every fight (it shows on the ammo icon next to the Repair Kit button); when it is 2 or less, an ammo refill from the bag is used right away (a reload in the middle of a fight costs a turn, and the creature attacks meanwhile), with a note on the map. If there is no refill in the bag the note says so. The setting "Refill the ammo after a fight" (on by default) turns it off.
- **Creature pop-up** (click a creature's picture): its stats in the game's own frame, a **Combat Evaluation** (a simulation of the fight from your stats and its stats: hit chances, hits to kill, extra attack / defense / damage, average hits, win / defeat chance, expected HP left), an attack button that fights in the background and shows the result and loot under the picture. Draggable by the title bar; Esc closes it.
- **Quick Links** (Modern layout): the game's quick links are small buttons in the map's top row, after the W and G buttons: the **backpack** (with how full it is, e.g. 12/15), **Quick Assault**, **Quick Buff**, **Medipack** and **Repair Kit**; the area map button is left out, since the map can be zoomed out to show all of it. The backpack opens the same backpack as on the profile page in a pop-up (folders and pages, the small checkboxes over the items, move / combine / split / destroy, use and equip, item size) with the components under it (a click destroys one, as in the game; the page links page through them), read from your profile page; messages show on top of it. The count on the button is read again after every fight, and from the world page right after you destroy or use an item in the pop-up. Assault, Medipack and Repair Kit are sent in the background and the game's message is shown in a dialog; the keys B, G, M and F do the same. The Medipack button shows under it how many medipacks of any size (every item with "Medipack" in its name, over all folders and pages) are in the bag, and the Repair Kit button how many repair items (Repair Bot, Repair Belt, Repair Drone, Diagnostic Kit, of any level: choosing a fitting one is up to you); it is counted when the page opens and again after you use a medipack or close the backpack. Next to the Repair Kit button an ammo icon (not a button) shows how many ammo refills in the bag fit your equipped weapon (Ammo items whose Ammo Type is the weapon's Damage Type, e.g. Crushing), counted at the same times. The counts under the buttons and the backpack count are coloured: green, yellow when running low (medipacks and ammo: 5 or less; the backpack: over 75% full) and red when out (0 left; the backpack full). Quick Buff keeps the game's own dialog.
- **Quests** open in a pop-up with the game's message, without leaving the page.
- **Healing** at a healer (the Heal button in the list) is sent in the background: the game's message ("You successfully healed at the cloning facility.") replaces the cost line in the list, the banner (HP, credits) is updated and the list is read again, without a page reload.
- **Mechanic**: the total repair bill and your credits shown next to the mechanic, and a **repair-all** button that works in place and updates the durability bar.
- After every fight or action the banner (HP, energy, credits, XP) is refreshed from the answer.

### Modern profile (switch at the bottom left)

The profile page rebuilt from its own markup with grid / flex, with the original pictures and the same positions and sizes, so nothing moves. Other players' profiles get the same frame. If anything cannot be read, that part keeps the game's own look, and if the whole build fails the original page stays.

- **Tidy-ups**: one border per section (the two columns no longer overlap), ten component slots instead of four, a backpack that fills its frame.
- **Backpack** (its own small app, no page reloads):
  - folder icons switch folders and page links change pages in place; the **last folder you used is remembered** and opens again after a page reload;
  - **refresh** button, **settings** button (item size 50 / 75 / 100 %, remembered);
  - a small see-through **checkbox in the corner of each item**; the actions **Move selected to folder**, **Combine**, **Split**, **Destroy** (asks for confirmation in a pop-up) appear only while something is selected;
  - **Ctrl+Click** on an item selects it instead of using or equipping it; **Shift+Click** selects every item from the last selected one to the clicked one (left to right, top to bottom), also in the pop-up bag of the world page;
  - clicking an item **uses** it (consumables, ammo, resources) with the game's message shown in the line above the bag (not in a pop-up, so you can keep using items); clicking gear **equips** it and refreshes the whole page content (stats, equipment, bag, buffs);
  - the bag and the components under it dim while they load; messages in a small line above the bag; the game's item tooltips on every item.
- **Components**: clicking a component destroys it (as in the game) and the page links page through them, all in place.
- **Equipment**: clicking an equipped item **unequips** it and refreshes the page content.
- **Combat Sets** pop-up: a searchable list of your saved sets with **Use** (equips it and refreshes the page), **Delete** (asks first) and **Save set** (name, then Confirm), with a green "Set saved." message.
- **Buffs, skills and energy** tooltips as described above.

### How it is delivered

- A tiny loader in Tampermonkey starts the last build it knew at once and checks GitHub Pages for a newer one in the background, so new versions reach you (from the next page) without updating anything, and in the Modern layout the game's own page stays hidden until the new layout is built, so you never see it for a moment first; if the site is down it uses the last build that worked.
- Walkable tiles, skill descriptions, stim buffs and the Guide's area numbers are data files, updated separately from the code.
