Sigmastorm Helper

Quality of life for Sigmastorm, made by Aurik16. Free and open source.

✓ The helper is installed

It loads itself on sigmastorm.com and keeps itself up to date: nothing more to do.

Reinstall the loader
1

Get a userscript manager

The helper needs Tampermonkey (a free browser extension). If you already have it, skip this step.

2

Install the helper

Click the button. Tampermonkey opens a tab asking you to confirm: press Install. No other action is needed; you do not have to update it again.

Install Sigmastorm Helper

If clicking only shows text, Tampermonkey is not installed or is turned off (step 1).

3

Open the game

Go to sigmastorm.com and reload the page. A small Original | Modern switch appears in the bottom left corner.

What it does

  • World map: a bigger map that zooms and drags, click a tile to walk there, footprints, quick combat from a floating action list.
  • Modern profile (switch at the bottom left): the profile rebuilt with modern CSS, same pictures. Backpack with folders, move / combine / split / destroy and quick use without reloading; equip and unequip without reloading; combat sets in a pop-up.
  • Tooltips: no more frozen page when hovering items, buff and skill tooltips show what they do (scaled to the level), a live energy countdown with the time it will be full.
  • Banner numbers, repair-all button at the mechanic, blueprint previews and more.

Good to know

  • It only runs on sigmastorm.com. Settings stay in your browser (localStorage); nothing is sent anywhere except to sigmastorm.com and this site.
  • The script updates itself within minutes of a new version being published.
  • Not affiliated with the game's owners. Use at your own risk; if the game changes something, the helper may need an update.
